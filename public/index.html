Raw activity is kept for your retention window; daily totals are kept forever, so your full history stays. Everything lives in your own Cloudflare account.

Keep WakaTime too (optional)

Your editor can only send to one server, so Waqt can pass every heartbeat on to WakaTime for you. In Cloudflare, open this Worker → Settings → Variables and Secrets → Add, choose Secret, name it WAKATIME_API_KEY and paste your key from wakatime.com/api-key. New activity then goes to both.

Import your WakaTime history

Bring your past coding time over from WakaTime. Only activity from before Waqt's first heartbeat (—) is imported, so nothing is counted twice. Your browser does the counting, then Waqt stores compact daily totals.

To import automatically, add your key from wakatime.com/api-key as the WAKATIME_API_KEY secret (Cloudflare → this Worker → Settings → Variables and Secrets). You can turn forwarding off below if you only want the import.

Backups

A backup holds your full history (the daily totals behind every chart, card and API answer) and your settings. Secrets such as report webhooks are left out, and so are raw heartbeats.

Automatic weekly backups: in your private GitHub copy of Waqt, add the secret WAQT_API_KEY (your key) and the variable WAQT_URL () under Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions. Every Sunday the Back up Waqt workflow saves one as a release, keeping the last 12.
