Connect your editors

  1. Install the free WakaTime plugin for your editor from wakatime.com/plugins (VS Code, Cursor, JetBrains, Vim, Neovim, Sublime, Xcode, terminals, browsers and more). You don't need a WakaTime account.
  2. Put this in ~/.wakatime.cfg (on Windows: %USERPROFILE%\.wakatime.cfg):
  3. Restart your editor and code for a couple of minutes. Your status bar shows today's time, and this dashboard fills in.
